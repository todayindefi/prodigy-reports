Areas for Improvement

  • All headline metrics declined week-over-week. Impressions down 20%, likes down 33%, engagement rate down 27%. The reduction in tweet volume partially explains this, but per-tweet performance (avg imp 101 vs 106) also dipped.
  • URL clicks fell to 2 — the lowest in recent weeks. Only 2 clicks from 4,850 impressions (0.04% CTR). The persistent click-through problem has worsened. No tweet generated more than 1 click.
  • Reply engagement is formulaic. The @bigjercrypto and @poppyduckk replies (15–30 imp range) follow a pattern of emoji-heavy, low-substance responses that contribute little to reach or engagement. They account for ~36 of 48 tweets but generated only ~3% of impressions.
  • Engagement question underperformed. “What’s stopping you from trying an on-chain fixed-yield vault?” (368 imp, 7.34% ER) — decent but no replies recorded, suggesting the question didn’t spark conversation as intended.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Experiment with link placement for CTR. Try embedding a prodigy.fi link directly into the top-performing tweet formats (TradFi comparisons, vault breakdowns) rather than relying on separate CTA tweets. At this follower scale, URLs in first tweets don’t penalize reach.
  • Reduce formulaic reply volume further. The emoji-heavy replies to the same 3–4 accounts are consuming ~75% of tweet volume for ~3% of impressions. Cut to 1–2 high-quality, substantive replies per day and reinvest that effort into original content.
  • Bring back engagement formats that drove replies. The engagement question got impressions but no replies. Try more opinionated framing: “most people earning yield on BTC right now are getting under 5%. what’s your number?” — give the audience something to react to, not just agree with.
  • Double down on the vault-specific breakdown format. The “$341 on 1 BTC” and “$371 on $100K” tweets are the most product-specific content that still performed well. Test making these a daily fixture with a consistent format and direct link.
  • Test a weekly thread series. Threads drove the best engagement in Jul 13–19 (the vault exit explainer at 845 imp). Launch a “Weekly Vault Watch” thread with market context → vault performance → specific opportunities → CTA.
