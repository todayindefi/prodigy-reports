What Went Well

  • Record-high average engagement rate of 22.6%. This is the highest weekly ER the account has ever posted, more than doubling last week’s 9.6% and surpassing the previous best of 17.2%. The audience is deeply resonating with the current content strategy.
  • Every single tweet exceeded 14% ER. The floor has risen dramatically — the “worst” performing tweet (14.19% ER) would have been a top performer in any previous week. Content quality is consistently high across all posts.
  • Likes per tweet jumped to 10.5 avg (+31% vs last week). Despite the post-V2 impression normalization, the audience that is seeing the content is engaging with it at a much higher rate. Quality over quantity is paying off.
  • Reposts consistently strong at 5–7 per tweet. At 5.8 reposts per tweet (+47% vs last week’s 4.0), shareability is at an all-time high. The educational and contrarian content formats are driving organic amplification.
  • Audience engagement deepening post-V2. The V2 launch attracted attention; now the content is converting that attention into genuine community engagement. The “USDC opportunity cost” tweet sparked 7 replies — the most discussion-generating format this week.

Areas for Improvement

  • Impressions have dropped back below pre-V2 levels. At 119 avg impressions per tweet, reach is now below even the 162 avg from two weeks ago. The V2 spike (712 avg) was temporary, and the account needs a new catalyst to grow reach.
  • Zero bookmarks for the third consecutive week. Despite highly engaging educational content (USDC cost analysis, autocallable explainer), nobody is saving tweets. The content sparks conversation but isn’t being treated as reference material.
  • Only 2 URL clicks across 6 tweets. Content is engaging but not converting to site visits. The audience is interacting with tweets in-feed (likes, replies, reposts) but not clicking through to Prodigy.fi. This is a funnel gap.
  • New follows limited to 1 this partial week. High engagement is not translating into follower growth. The content is resonating with existing followers but not reaching enough new eyes to drive acquisition.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Double down on the question-based engagement format. The “what did holding USDC actually cost you” tweet drove 7 replies and a 31% ER — the highest of the week. More “did you know” and “what if” framing will continue to spark discussions and boost reply counts.
  • Add CTAs and links to educational threads. The content is generating strong engagement but near-zero clicks. Pair the educational tweets with a clear “see how this works in a vault” link or CTA to convert engagement into site traffic.
  • Maintain this ER level while working to grow reach back. The 22.6% ER is exceptional — do not sacrifice engagement quality for impressions. Instead, pursue collaborations, quote-tweet exchanges, and strategic replies to larger accounts to expand reach organically.
  • Create a bookmark-worthy thread or infographic. Three weeks of zero bookmarks suggests the content format needs adjustment. A numbered “5 things you didn’t know about structured yield” thread or a visual comparison chart would be more save-worthy.
  • Target 2 tweets per day consistently through a full 7-day week. This partial week had only 6 tweets over 3 days (2/day pace). Sustaining that cadence through a full week would yield ~14 tweets with potentially strong aggregate numbers given the current ER.
