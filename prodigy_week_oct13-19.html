Areas for Improvement

  • Reply volume is dragging the headline numbers. Avg ER fell to 3.1% from 5.1% and avg impressions to 86 from 110, even though originals alone averaged 9.1% ER and 378 impressions.
  • Bookmarks fell 24% to 66 from last week’s record 87. Still the second-best week on record, but no single post broke 10 saves the way the oracle explainer and settlement examples did last week.
  • Total impressions slipped 6% to 5,076. Reach is flat at roughly 400 per original; with no launch or milestone this week there was nothing to push a post past 500.
  • High-save posts still lacked a next step. The “no early exit” (8 bookmarks), rate-hike odds (7) and October seasonality (6) posts earned 0 clicks between them.

Recommendations for Next Week
