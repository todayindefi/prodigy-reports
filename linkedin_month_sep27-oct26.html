What Went Well

  • The CPI + live rates post carried the month. 1,850 impressions, 15 clicks and 1,395 unique impressions that week — 81% of all impressions and 63% of all clicks in the period. A news hook people were already watching, followed by specific vault rates, is the format that works here.
  • LinkedIn posts have a long tail. The CPI post kept earning impressions for five days and peaked on day three (777). On X a post is mostly done within a day, so LinkedIn rewards fewer, stronger posts.
  • Launch posts drive clicks. The NVDAx launch video earned 7 clicks from 192 impressions (3.6% CTR), the best click rate of any post.
  • The people who see posts engage with them. The gold launch hit 30.8% ER and the NVDAx Buy Low post 7.6%, both with comments and reposts. The weak point is reach, not the copy.

Areas for Improvement

  • The gold launch reached 26 people. On X the same launch got 1,762 impressions. On LinkedIn it seems to have gone out without reshares from the team.
  • The latest week (Oct 20–26) was nearly dormant. 198 impressions, 37 unique viewers and 1 click, down from 1,916 impressions the week before.
  • Only 4 posts in 4 weeks, all from one person. Nothing was posted between launches apart from the one rates post, so the page has no regular rhythm for LinkedIn to distribute.
  • Very few page visitors. 56 unique visitors (137 page views) all month, from three reported locations (Taipei, Kaohsiung, Bengaluru). Entry-level viewers are half the seniority breakdown, so a good share is likely the team's own network rather than investors.
  • No follower data. The followers export wasn't downloaded, so follower growth can't be measured yet.

Recommendations

  • Make news + live rates a weekly fixture. CPI, FOMC, jobs reports, big ETF flow days: copy the Oct 14 structure (what happened → why it moves option yields → today's rates with expiries).
  • Post 2–3 times a week, adapting the best X explainers. The limit order vs buy-low comparison, the “every market number is missing one of these” checklist and the gold dialogue post suit a finance and tech audience.
  • Have the founder and team reshare every post with a line of their own. Company-page reach depends on that first push. The 26-impression gold launch shows what happens without it.
  • Write for the audience that's actually there. Software, financial services and blockchain professionals: lead with mechanics and risk (no lending, no liquidation, fixed rate at deposit) rather than crypto-native shorthand.
  • Download the followers export next time. It's under the Followers tab of page analytics, and it would let this report track follower growth like the X reports do.
