  • Posting cadence tripled from 4 to 12 tweets, reversing the downtrend. The account had been slipping (8 → 6 → 4 tweets over the previous weeks). This week delivered on the recommendation to increase volume, and every metric responded positively.
  • Likes quadrupled (14 → 57) and reposts 5x’d (3 → 16). These are the strongest engagement numbers in over a month. The combination of higher volume and stronger content drove outsized results across the board.
  • The “peace deal” tweet (114 imp, 10.53% ER) shows geopolitical-market takes drive engagement. Tying crypto strategy to real-world macro events gives the account a timely, authoritative voice that followers want to engage with.

Areas for Improvement

  • Only 2 URL clicks across 12 tweets — the viral PMUSD tweet (1,591 imp) had ZERO links. This is the biggest missed opportunity of the week. A breakout tweet with no link or CTA means all that reach generated zero direct traffic to prodigy.fi.
  • The hardware wallet tweet (154 imp, 1.30% ER) got wide reach but almost no engagement. Generic security advice doesn’t resonate with this audience. Reach without engagement suggests the algorithm pushed it but the content didn’t hook readers.
  • Three tweets on May 7 (Thu) diluted reach. The link-only tweet got just 8 impressions, and the “ETH break up/down” tweet landed at only 29 impressions. The third tweet of the day consistently gets buried by the algorithm.
  • The “ETH break up/down” tweet (29 imp, 17.24% ER) flopped on reach despite high engagement rate. Good content suppressed by over-posting on the same day. The high ER from the small audience that saw it suggests this would have performed well on its own.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • When a tweet goes viral, immediately reply to it with a product link/CTA. The PMUSD tweet hit 1,591 impressions with zero clicks because there was no link. A self-reply with “This is why we built transparent yield at prodigy.fi — [link]” would have captured meaningful traffic.
  • Keep the opinionated/warning format — this is the founder’s strongest voice. “Here’s what’s risky, here’s why” tweets outperform generic advice by a wide margin. The PMUSD tweet proves the audience responds to a founder who calls things out.
  • Reduce to 2 tweets/day max. May 7 had 3 tweets and the 3rd flopped at 8 impressions. Two well-timed tweets per day is the sweet spot — more than that and the algorithm suppresses later posts.
  • Always include a link when mentioning Prodigy products. Several tweets mentioned prodigy.fi vaults or Pro Earn without a clickable link. Every product mention should have a direct URL and a clear CTA.
  • Develop the Pro Earn angle with specific yield examples. “Eye popping yields” is vague. Tweets with concrete numbers (e.g., “35% APY on a 1-day ETH vault”) have historically driven the most clicks and engagement. Show, don’t tell.
