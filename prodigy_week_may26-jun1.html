Areas for Improvement

  • URL clicks dropped 50% to just 4 — the lowest in over a month. Only 1 tweet earned a click (the capital protected notes post). The thread-reply CTA tweets (“bankless profits”, “linked for you”, “your chance here”, “no requirements”) are correctly structured as second tweets in threads to avoid Twitter’s link penalty on the main tweet, but generated 0 clicks combined despite averaging 72 impressions. The thread strategy is sound — the CTA copy needs work.
  • Average engagement rate dipped to 8.7% from 10.2%. Reply tweet dilution continues — 22 reply tweets averaging 4 impressions each pull the averages down. Original content averaged ~15.5% ER, which is healthy.
  • Follower count declined from 11.6K to 11.5K. Net loss of ~100 followers despite increased activity. High volume of low-quality replies may be causing unfollows from users who see the feed as noisy.
  • Thread-reply CTAs all use generic, interchangeable copy. “Bankless profits”, “linked for you”, “your chance here”, “no requirements” — these second-tweet CTAs all point to the same URL with vague copy that doesn’t connect to the specific thread above them. The thread structure is smart (avoids link penalty on the main tweet), but the CTA copy should reference the thread’s topic to give readers a reason to click.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Make thread-reply CTAs specific to each thread’s topic. The thread structure is correct — keeping links out of the first tweet avoids Twitter’s algorithmic penalty. But all four CTA replies used generic copy (“linked for you”, “your chance here”) and earned 0 clicks combined. Try topic-specific CTAs instead: after the capital protected notes thread, “set up capital protection on your BTC here”; after the ETH thread, “buy ETH at your target price”. Match the CTA to the hook that just earned their attention.
  • Keep the TradFi-comparison format as the weekly anchor. Three weeks running, this format leads in impressions, likes, and bookmarks. Aim for 2 per week, spaced 3–4 days apart. Potential topics: reverse convertibles, autocallables, dual currency deposits.
