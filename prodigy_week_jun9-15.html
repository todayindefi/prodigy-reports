What Went Well

  • The lending protocols comparison tweet hit 2,621 impressions — by far the biggest single tweet in ProdigyFi’s tracked history. This is a variation of the TradFi comparison format, but comparing against DeFi lending instead. The format “X gives you Y, but here’s the catch” continues to dominate.
  • Avg impressions surged 60% to 150, driven by higher quality content with fewer tweets (29 vs 45). Quality over quantity is clearly winning.
  • Total impressions rose to 4,352 despite posting 36% fewer tweets — the best weekly total in the entire tracking period.
  • Engagement questions are performing consistently: “what’s your real win rate” (133 imp, 21.8% ER, 6 bkmrks) and the ETH Fear & Greed tweets drive replies and bookmarks.
  • The “friend conversation” format (191 imp, 17.8% ER, 9 replies) is a new winner — personal storytelling that makes the product the punchline. Similar to the dinner anecdote from May.
  • Bookmarks stayed strong at 48. Nearly every original tweet earned 4+ bookmarks. The content is consistently reference-worthy.

Areas for Improvement

  • URL clicks dropped to 5 (from 7). Only the breakout 2,621 imp tweet earned 3 clicks, and the ETH Fear & Greed tweet earned 1. Most tweets still earn 0 clicks.
  • Likes dropped 14% despite higher impressions. The breakout tweet had only a 0.76% like rate (20 likes on 2,621 imp). Algorithmic distribution is reaching new audiences who aren’t engaging proportionally.
  • Reposts dropped significantly from 78 to 53. Fewer tweets were share-worthy despite higher reach.
  • No thread-reply CTA was posted after the 2,621 imp breakout — a missed opportunity to capture that audience with a link while they were engaged.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Always post a thread-reply CTA within minutes of any tweet that breaks 200+ impressions. The 2,621 imp tweet had no follow-up link, leaving clicks on the table from people who had to find the site themselves.
  • Keep the DeFi-comparison format as a new rotation alongside TradFi comparisons. “Lending protocols give you X, Prodigy gives you Y” works as well as the bank comparison format and hits a more native audience.
  • Continue the “friend conversation” / personal storytelling format. 191 imp and 9 replies shows this drives the most conversation of any format.
  • Focus on quality over quantity. 29 tweets at 150 avg imp beat 45 tweets at 94 avg imp. Fewer, better tweets are clearly the right strategy.
  • Add specific vault APY numbers to Fear & Greed tweets. “Fear & Greed: 12 → ETH buy low vaults at 45% APY” is more actionable than abstract statements about premiums spiking.
