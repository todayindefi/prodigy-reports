  • The gold buy-low question was the week's top post. “would you buy gold 31% below its january high?” did 485 impressions, 5 bookmarks and 2 clicks at 8.0% ER. A specific price ($3,880), a specific rate (8%) and a question in the first line.

Areas for Improvement

  • Impressions fell 27% to 5,367 (account-level −14% to 7,428). There was no breakout post: the best did 485, against 1,762 for last week's gold launch.
  • 51 of 64 tweets were replies, and 48 of them went out on Oct 31. Together they earned 670 impressions (about 13 each). One batch day like that drags the averages without reaching anyone new.
  • Still losing followers: 4 follows vs 11 unfollows. The scraped count slipped from 10.8K to 10.7K, and profile visits stayed flat at 49.
  • Bookmarks dropped a third week in a row: 46, down from 64 and 67. Explainers still earn saves, but there were fewer reference-style posts this week.
  • Clicks depended on one post. 10 of the 14 came from the dividend post. The October recap (360 impressions) and the $84K cbBTC vault post (293) each got 1, because the link or rate came late in the copy.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Make “X is a decision, a vault is a rate” contrasts a weekly format. The dividend post drove 70% of the week's clicks. Try the same shape on coupons, staking yield, savings accounts and CDs, with the vault link in the same tweet.
  • Repeat the “would you buy X% below its high?” question with live rates. It worked for gold. Run it for BTC and ETH after drawdowns, with the real price, rate and expiry.
  • Spread replies across the week and point them at bigger accounts' news posts. No more 48-reply days. Ten well-placed replies under large accounts' midterm, jobs-report and hack threads reach more new people than a batch to the usual circle.
  • Plan one big post a week. Launches and news posts break out (gold launch 1,762). Candidates for next week: a payrolls-day rates post and a “hacks since Binance” custody explainer.
  • Quote-tweet @0xYield_Maxi's best posts. The founder's Vaults of the Week (910) and macro calendar (1,431 on Nov 3) both outperformed everything on @ProdigyFi this week.
