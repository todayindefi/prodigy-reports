What Went Well

  • Bookmarks surged to 70 — up 63% from 43 last week. This is a significant signal: the audience is saving content for reference. The “linked price vs limit order” explainer (9 bkmrks), “structured yield vault” tweet (10 bkmrks), and “premiums priced low for a quarter” tweet (8 bkmrks) drove the most saves. Educational content about vault mechanics is bookmark-worthy.
  • Per-tweet performance improved across the board. Avg impressions up 11% (117 vs 105), avg ER up 13% (3.5% vs 3.1%), with fewer tweets (45 vs 48). Quality over quantity continues to pay off.
  • Product education tweets drove the best engagement. The “linked price is not a limit order” tweet (312 imp, 9.62% ER, 9 bkmrks, 14 likes) and “structured yield vault” tweet (403 imp, 6.95% ER, 10 bkmrks, 16 likes) were the top engagement performers. Explaining how the product actually works outperforms generic CTA content.
  • Market context tweets maintained strong reach. ETH vs BTC comparison (538 imp), weak BTC volume (470 imp), and Citadel/Fed take (400 imp) all broke 400 impressions. Tying market data to vault positioning continues to work.

Areas for Improvement

  • URL clicks dropped to 1 — the worst on record. Only 1 click from 5,269 impressions (0.02% CTR). This has been a persistent problem but hit a new low. The content is engaging but not converting to site visits at all.
  • Likes and replies declined vs 2 weeks ago. Likes: 122 this week vs 168 two weeks ago (−27%). Replies: 51 vs 83 (−39%). The week-over-week trend is positive (up from last week) but the broader trajectory from Jul 13–19 is still down.
  • Reply tweets still account for ~73% of volume but ~3% of impressions. 33 of 45 tweets are replies to the same 5 accounts, averaging 18 impressions each. The ratio hasn’t improved.
  • Zero clicks on top-performing tweets. The top 4 tweets by impressions (538, 470, 453, 425 combined = 1,886 imp) generated 0 URL clicks between them. High-reach content isn’t driving any traffic to the product.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Add direct links to top-performing tweet formats. The market context and product education tweets are getting 400–500 impressions with 0 clicks. Embed a prodigy.fi link directly into these tweets — at this follower scale, links don’t suppress reach.
  • Build a weekly “vault explainer” series around the bookmark winners. The linked-price and structured-vault tweets earned 9–10 bookmarks each. Create a recurring format: one concept explained per tweet, with a specific vault example and link. These are the tweets people want to reference.
  • Continue reducing reply volume. Cut the formulaic replies to 1–2 substantive ones per day. Reallocate that effort to original content — the data consistently shows original tweets drive 97% of impressions.
  • Test a “vault receipt” tweet on the ProdigyFi account. The Yield Maxi account saw strong engagement (134 imp, 11.19% ER) with a follow-up tweet reporting that a previously-shilled vault expired profitably. Replicate this format on the project account.
