What Went Well

  • Fewer posts, more reach per post: 15 tweets averaged 345 impressions, up from 89. Replies fell to 5 from 51, which was last report's top fix. Total tweet impressions were 5,173 (−9%), and account-level impressions held flat at 7,382 (7,428 the week before).
  • The AAPLx launch week worked as a series. Six Apple posts did about 2,300 impressions between them. The foldable post (“Apple’s $1,999 foldable sold out in a day. you’ve already decided what that’s worth”) was the week's top post: 817 impressions, 4 clicks, 4 bookmarks. It opened with a fact everyone had seen, then turned it into a price decision.
  • Dollar framing beat percentages. “pick $160 as your buy price… $100,000 earns $689” did 770 impressions and 2 clicks; “$11 or $164?” (savings account vs an AAPLx vault) did 288 with a click.
  • The weekend NVDAx post was the best click converter. “nvidia’s board opened an investigation on sunday, with wall street closed. on-chain, nvidia kept trading” earned 4 clicks on 283 impressions (1.4%). It's an angle only a tokenized-stock product has.
  • Explainers still earn saves. The NVDAx voting-rights explainer (354, 4 bookmarks) and the ETH liquidation sell-high post (344, 5 bookmarks) led the 42 bookmarks.

Areas for Improvement

  • Followers: 1 follow, 16 unfollows, and the scraped count slipped to 10.6K. Profile visits fell to 36. The account is reaching people but not giving them a reason to follow.
  • Clicks dipped to 12 from 14, and 10 of them came from three posts. Seven of the ten top posts got no clicks, mostly because they had no link or the link sat in a reply (“read more on the launch here” did 28 impressions).
  • Total impressions fell 9%, with no breakout. The best post did 817, against 1,762 for the gold launch two weeks ago.
  • Weekend reach was thin: 580 and 790 account impressions on Nov 7–8, the two lowest days of the week, with the most unfollows on Sunday.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Make “the stock market is closed, NVDAx isn’t” a standing format. Weekend and after-hours headlines (Nvidia this week) are when tokenized stocks are the only place to act. Post the on-chain move and a vault rate within hours.
  • Lead with dollars, not APY. “$100,000 earns $689” and “$11 or $164?” were among the week's best. Use the same framing for the Micron launch.
  • Put the link in the first tweet, not a reply. Three of the ten top posts produced all the clicks, and all three had the link in the post.
  • Give people a reason to follow. Pin a short “what we post” thread (weekly rates, tokenized-stock launches, market-closed moves), and end one post a day with a reason to come back (“results Friday”).
  • Plan the Micron launch as a series, like AAPLx. Launch post, then a memory-cycle explainer, a dollar-framed rate post and an earnings-week post (mid-December). Quote-tweet the founder's Vaults of the Week on Mondays.
