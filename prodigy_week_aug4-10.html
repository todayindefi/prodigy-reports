What Went Well

  • Impressions exploded to 14,029 — up 132% and the best week since early March. Avg impressions per tweet nearly tripled (468 vs 134). The algorithm is distributing content well beyond the follower base.
  • Macro-anchored content drove breakout reach. The Fed/payrolls tweet (2,586 imp), S&P 200-week chart tweet (2,466 imp), and ETF inflows tweet (1,505 imp) all broke 1,000+ impressions. Tying vault positioning to real-world events is the proven formula.
  • The “win rate” dialogue tweet showed personality works on the brand account. 2,068 impressions on a creative, almost meme-like format proves ProdigyFi can have a voice beyond data and education.
  • URL clicks jumped 600% to 7. Specific vault examples with dollar amounts (“$362 on 20 ETH,” “$430 on $100K”) drove clicks. The “sell ETH at $2,000” tweet (819 imp, 2 clicks) and “linked price sets your rate” tweet (652 imp, 2 clicks) were the top converters.
  • Tweet volume reduced to 30 (from 45) with 2.3x the impressions. The quality-over-quantity thesis is now proven at scale for the project account.

Areas for Improvement

  • Engagement rate dropped to 1.9% — lowest in weeks. This is the expected tradeoff of high-reach content hitting cold audiences who don’t engage. Not necessarily bad, but worth monitoring if it persists.
  • Bookmarks dropped from 70 to 44. Last week’s product explainer content (linked price vs limit order) was more save-worthy. This week’s macro content gets consumed but not referenced.
  • Likes flat at 110 despite 2.3x impressions. The new audience reached this week is watching but not engaging yet — typical for breakout weeks.
  • Top 4 tweets by impressions (7,625 combined) generated 0 URL clicks. The highest-reach content still isn’t converting to site visits. The macro tweets that break out don’t include product links.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Keep tweet volume at ~30/week (4–5/day). The data is definitive: fewer, higher-quality tweets massively outperform high-volume posting. This week proved it at 2.3x the reach with 33% fewer tweets.
  • Embed links in macro tweets. The top-performing format (macro event + vault positioning) gets the reach but zero clicks. Add a prodigy.fi link directly into these tweets — at 11.3K followers, URL suppression is negligible.
  • Mix personality formats into the rotation. The “win rate” dialogue (2,068 imp) was the 3rd highest tweet and the most distinctive. 1–2 personality/creative tweets per week alongside the data-driven content keeps the feed from feeling like a terminal.
  • Continue the specific-vault-with-dollar-amounts format. “$362 on 20 ETH” (2 clicks) and “$430 on $100K” (2 clicks) drove the most conversions. These outperform generic “earn yield” CTAs every time.
  • Reply volume still at 60% — push toward 40%. 18 of 30 tweets are replies averaging 19 impressions. Cut to 2 substantive replies per day and reinvest in original content.
