  • Long gaps between posts. Nothing went out from Oct 30 to Nov 1 (35 impressions across the three days), while X posted daily. The export ends Nov 2, so the midterm days will show in the next one.
  • No followers or visitors export this time, so follower growth and who is visiting can't be compared with the last report.
