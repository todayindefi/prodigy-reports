  • The gold follow-ups kept the launch alive for three days. The dialogue-format post (“you get paid to hold gold now?”) hit 478 impressions at 7.7% ER, and the “no gold dealer or $10,000” accessibility post did 441 impressions, 9.1% ER and 8 reposts. The dialogue format is new and worth repeating.
  • Concrete worked examples still drive the clicks. The 10-ounce XAUT sell-high at $4,080 example earned 2 clicks (the week's best) at 9.75% ER, and the limit-order vs buy-low comparison earned 1 click at 8.2% ER.

Areas for Improvement

  • Net −18 followers in launch week. 2 new follows against 20 unfollows, and the scraped count slipped from 10.9K to 10.8K. Profile visits fell to 46 from 51 even though account impressions rose 25%, so the launch reach didn't turn into profile interest.
  • URL clicks fell to 6 from 11, the lowest in three weeks. The launch post earned 1 click on 1,762 impressions (0.06%). The two thread replies under it — a link-only reply (305 imp, 0 clicks) and “more details here:” (39 imp, 0 clicks) — gave no reason to click and repeated each other.
  • Bookmarks dropped for a second week: 58, down from 67 and 91. The mix tilted towards announcement and promo copy and away from the settlement and yield-source explainers that earned saves in early October.
  • 40 of 55 tweets were replies, almost all to the same five accounts. They were posted in back-to-back batches, earned 688 impressions combined (about 17 each) and almost no engagement. They pull the average ER down to 3.0% without reaching new people.
  • Two posts were bare links with no copy. The Oct 20 link-only post (336 imp, 1 click) and the launch-thread link reply relied on the card preview alone.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Run a second wave on gold built around worked examples. The $4,080 XAUT example was the click leader. Post one live-rate XAUT buy-low or sell-high example each week with the link in the first tweet.
  • Use one CTA reply per thread, written for that thread. Replace “more details here:” and bare links with copy that points back to the parent, for example “the $4,080 sell-high above is live here →”.
  • Bring back 2–3 bookmark-worthy explainers a week. Settlement and oracle mechanics, yield-source comparisons and buy-low vs limit order have the best save rates; the checklist format (11.0% ER) is a good template.
  • Move reply time from the same five accounts to larger accounts' posts on gold, FOMC and tokenized stocks. Replies that land in front of new audiences can grow followers. The current batches don't, and the week ended at net −18.
  • Keep the FOMC and midterms rate-odds series going, with a vault bridge. Rate-odds posts were in the top two last week (657 imp) and held at 354 this week. Pair the next one with the BTC buy-low and sell-high rates around the decision.
