Areas for Improvement

  • Average engagement rate dropped to 5.4% from 7.8%. The high-impression tweets (1,471 and 597) had very low ERs (3.2% and 1.84%). Reach is expanding but engagement isn’t scaling with it — the new audience these tweets reach isn’t converting to likes and reposts at the same rate as the core following.
  • Thread-reply CTA tweets continue to underperform on clicks. Two CTA thread replies (“If you want higher yield…” at 54 imp, “access institutional yield” at 20 imp) earned 0 clicks combined. The thread structure is correct for avoiding link penalties, but the generic copy doesn’t give readers a reason to click.
  • No posting on Thursday or Saturday. Two gap days mean lost algorithmic momentum. The account posted only on Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, and Sun — missing Fri and Sat entirely.
  • Bookmarks flat at 3 for the second consecutive week. Only the DeFi risk tweet, the “insider” tweet, and the consolidation tweet earned saves. The market commentary tweets — despite high reach — aren’t producing reference-worthy content that people want to return to.

Recommendations for Next Week

  • Double down on the “risk breakdown” format. The DeFi risk tweet (1,471 imp) was this account’s best performer in weeks. List specific, named risks in competing products, then contrast with Prodigy’s structure. Try: impermanent loss, smart contract exploit history, governance token dilution.

  • Post every day — even if just a short market take. Two gap days (Fri, Sat) break algorithmic momentum. A one-sentence ETH or BTC reaction keeps the feed alive and costs almost nothing in effort.
  • Pair market commentary with a vault tie-in. The ETH sentiment tweet (597 imp) and ETH believers tweet (396 imp) had massive reach but low engagement and zero clicks. Adding a single line like “earning 30% APY on ETH while I wait for direction” turns commentary into a soft conversion funnel.
